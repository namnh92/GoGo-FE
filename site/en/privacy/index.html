GoGo privacy policy

Applies to the GoGo app on iOS and Android, and to gogo.id.vn.

In effect from ⟦legal.effectiveDate⟧ · Tiếng Việt →

1. Who is responsible

GoGo is a personal project with no registered company. The person responsible for your data is ⟦legal.ownerName⟧. Privacy questions go to namnh@gogo.id.vn.

GoGo does not send: contacts, calendar, messages, health data, payment details, passwords for other services, and it does not read your location while the app is in the background.

Tapping “Directions” opens Google Maps with the destination address. You start that yourself and GoGo sends nothing more — but Google receives the address through your browser or Maps app.

3. What the libraries inside the app collect on their own

Four third-party libraries run inside the app and collect data by themselves, even where GoGo writes no code to send any:

  • OneSignal (push notifications) — per their documentation: operating system, language, timezone, country, app version, device model, package name, carrier, IP address, push token, plus session data and the notification permission state. GoGo does not enable the OneSignal location feature.
  • Tenjin (install attribution) — per their documentation: device identifiers, IP address, device type and model, locale, country, platform and OS version, bundle id, SDK and app version, the Limit Ad Tracking setting, and Apple Search attribution if enabled. On Android the build declares the advertising-id permission and includes the App Set ID and the Play Install Referrer. On iOS there is no tracking prompt, so Tenjin does not receive the Apple advertising identifier; only the vendor identifier and the IP address.
  • Sentry (error reporting) — device, OS and app context attached by the SDK. The projects are configured not to store IP addresses and not to store a location inferred from one. No performance tracing, no session replay, no screenshots, and no view hierarchy.
  • The Google Maps SDK (the in-app map, used on iOS as well as Android) — Google receives the API key, device and app information, the IP address, the requested map region and the coordinates of the pins, which are the places in your plan. The app turns off the part of the SDK that reads the device location.

4. Permissions the app asks for on your device

  • Location while using the app — to suggest places near you. Optional: you can pick an area by hand.
  • Photo library — to add an avatar and check-in photos.
  • Notifications — to tell you someone joined, a vote finished, or it is time to go.

The app does not ask for: background location, the camera, contacts, the calendar, or the microphone.

5. Who receives your data

  • GoGo servers (api-*.gogo.id.vn) — where your account, rooms, plans and content live.
  • OneSignal — delivers push notifications on behalf of GoGo.
  • Tenjin — measures where an install came from. GoGo declares Tenjin a third party receiving shared data rather than a processor acting only for GoGo: it receives device identifiers and install or app-open events.
  • Sentry — receives error reports and session data so GoGo can fix bugs.
  • Google Maps Platform — draws the in-app map, and computes travel time: GoGo servers send the starting point you chose along with the coordinates of the stops.

The infrastructure GoGo runs on processes data for GoGo too: the PostgreSQL database at Neon, the Redis cache at Upstash, images and files at Cloudflare R2, and Cloudflare for the edge network. Secrets live in AWS SSM and contain no personal data.

6. How long it is kept

  • Account data: as long as the account exists, then as section 8 describes.
  • Precise starting-point coordinates: deleted 30 days after the room ends. An address you typed yourself stays.
  • The text you type when searching: 90 days (after that only the counts remain).
  • Sign-in attempt records: 30 days.
  • Guest sessions that were never registered: revoked and anonymized 7 days after they expire.
  • An export file you asked for: deleted 24 hours after it is ready, and downloadable once.
  • The record of a privacy request: 12 months after it closes, as evidence it was handled.
  • A job runs daily to carry the above out.

7. Security

Passwords are not stored in a readable form: GoGo stores an Argon2id hash. The signed-in session on your device sits in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore.

8. Your rights

  • Export your data — in the app: Profile → ⚙️ Account → Your data → Export my data (up to 3 times an hour). The file can only be downloaded from your own session, which is why GoGo does not email it.
  • Delete your account — in the app: Profile → ⚙️ Account → Delete account. If you cannot open the app, see the data deletion page.
  • Correct your data — edit your profile in the app; optional fields can be left empty.
  • Withdraw a permission — location, photos and notifications can be turned off in your device settings; the notification switch is in the app as well.
  • Anything else — write to namnh@gogo.id.vn; the request is recorded as section 6 describes.

9. This website

gogo.id.vn is a static site on Cloudflare Workers. It sets no cookies, has no analytics, no tracking scripts, and loads nothing from anyone else servers — the webfont is served from this domain. The Vietnamese home page carries one small inline script that reads the URL only, to send old ?lang=en links to the English version. Cloudflare processes IP addresses and request data to serve and protect the site, and may set a technical cookie when it shows a security check.

10. When this policy changes

A new version is posted at this address with a new effective date. GoGo has no in-app or email notice mechanism, so it does not promise one. The full revision history is public: the history of this page on GitHub.
