Delete your GoGo account and data

This page is for GoGo app accounts — above all when you can no longer open the app.

Tiếng Việt →

1. Fastest: delete it in the app

Open GoGo → Profile → ⚙️ Account → Delete account, then confirm. It runs immediately; you do not wait for anyone to reply.

2. If you cannot open the app: send an email

Write from the email address you registered with to namnh@gogo.id.vn. The button below opens a message with the subject and body ready; fill in your registered address and send it.

Open the deletion request

What the message needs to say:

  • the email address you registered with;
  • that you are asking for your account and personal data to be deleted;
  • that you understand reviews and contributed photos stay on GoGo without your name;
  • if you remember it: your display name in the app.

3. How GoGo verifies it is you

If the message comes from the account email address, the request is processed. If it comes from another address, GoGo deletes nothing — the request is recorded as not identity-verified and the reply says so, with instructions to reset your password and delete the account in the app. GoGo accepts no other form of proof, because there is no way to check one.

4. What is removed and what stays

Removed

  • the way in: your email address and password;
  • every session and every device push registration;
  • display name, avatar, home area, usual budget, date of birth, interests;
  • saved items, notifications and notification settings;
  • your name on rooms you took part in, replaced by a fixed anonymous label.

Kept, deliberately

  • a technical record of the account (its id only), so data links do not break and the id is never reissued to someone else;
  • reviews you wrote;
  • photos you contributed to places.

The last two are content you contributed for other people to read, so they stay — without your name on them.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you asked for a data export and have not downloaded it, that file is revoked too.

5. Data already held by providers

After deletion GoGo no longer sends you notifications, because the push registrations are gone. But data OneSignal, Tenjin or Sentry already received sits in their systems under their policies — GoGo does not delete it there on your behalf.

6. The record of your request

GoGo keeps a record of the request itself — its type, the dates, the email address you wrote from, and the outcome — for 12 months after it closes, as evidence that it was handled. That record deliberately outlives the account, and it never contains your exported data.

7. How long it takes

GoGo acknowledges the request within 72 hours and completes it within 30 days, then replies to the same address the request came from.

8. If you joined a room as a guest

A guest has no account. A guest session is revoked and anonymized automatically 7 days after it expires, so there is nothing to request.

9. If you want your data before deleting

Exporting is only possible in the app: Profile → ⚙️ Account → Your data → Export data. The file can only be downloaded from your own session and GoGo does not email it — which is why this page only takes deletion requests.
