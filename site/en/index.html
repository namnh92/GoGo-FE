No more “so… where do we go?”

Planning an outing usually takes an evening of messages, and someone still ends up going along with the majority. GoGo lets couples and friend groups each choose, vote together, and get one shared itinerary that fits the budget and the time.

Screenshots from our internal test build (iOS), using test data. The interface may change before release. The app’s interface is in Vietnamese.

What makes it different

Built for a group decision

Not another list of venues — a way for the whole group to reach one plan.

🗳️

Everyone gets a say

Each person chooses on their own; the result is a genuine overlap, not the loudest voice. In a group, the host makes the final call.

⚖️

Balanced preferences

Suggestions are scored on the whole group’s preferences, distance and spend by a rule-based algorithm, and always say why they were picked.

💰

A clear budget

Budget per group or per person. Prices always carry their unit; when there is no data, the app says “No price information” instead of guessing.

🗺️

One shared itinerary

Stops are ordered with travel time between them. Locked stops always stay when the itinerary is regenerated.

How it works

From “let’s hang out” to heading home

  1. Create a room

    Open a room for the outing, set budget and time.

  2. Invite friends

    Send one link and everyone joins the room.

  3. Share preferences

    Each person says what they want to eat, do and spend.

  4. Vote

    Everyone chooses on their own; GoGo finds the group’s overlap.

  5. Get the itinerary

    Stops are arranged into one sensible plan.

  6. Go out & review

    Follow the plan, then share how it went.

Progress

Current stage: finishing the MVP

Done so far

  • iOS and Android app covering the full flow: create a room, invite, preferences, vote, itinerary, the outing itself and review.
  • The full flow has run on real iOS and Android phones against our test environment; system-wide integration testing is in progress.
  • An internal tool for managing the place catalogue and moderating content.

Next (planned)

  • A closed trial with a first group of users.
  • Release on the App Store and Google Play.
  • Grow the place dataset.

About the project

  • A self-funded Vietnamese startup project; no legal entity has been registered yet.
  • The product is not released, so there are no external users and no revenue yet.
  • Planned, unvalidated revenue model: partnerships with venues (offers and visibility at the moment a group is choosing) and a premium tier for users.
  • Vietnamese is the default language; English is available.
